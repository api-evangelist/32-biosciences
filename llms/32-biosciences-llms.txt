# 32 Biosciences

> 32 Biosciences ("Three Squared") is a North Chicago, Illinois gastrointestinal biotechnology
> company founded in 2023, pioneering gut mucosal-immune science to prevent and treat GI disease.
> It runs a GI Discovery Platform measuring metabolomic signatures of gut mucosal-immune function
> and a CS Therapeutic Platform whose lead asset CS-0003 is a Mucosal Immune Modulator.
